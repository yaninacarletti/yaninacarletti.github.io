## Business Context

Customer retention is one of the most important challenges for subscription-based businesses. Acquiring a new customer is generally more expensive than retaining an existing one, making churn prediction a valuable business capability.

The objective of this project is to identify customers with a high probability of leaving the service, enabling proactive retention strategies.

---

## Challenge

The available dataset contains customer behavior over time, including product usage, activity frequency and subscription information.

The main challenges were:

- Cleaning inconsistent records.
- Building meaningful behavioral features.
- Handling class imbalance.
- Selecting evaluation metrics aligned with business objectives.
- Explaining model predictions.

---

## Solution

The project follows an end-to-end machine learning workflow.
