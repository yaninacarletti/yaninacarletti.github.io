## Business Context

Many organizations accumulate large amounts of customer information but lack a clear understanding of how different customer profiles behave.

Instead of applying identical marketing actions to every customer, segmentation enables companies to identify homogeneous groups and design personalized strategies for each one.

---

## Challenge

The main challenge was to transform a multidimensional customer dataset into meaningful business segments while preserving interpretability.

Since there were no predefined labels, the project required an unsupervised learning approach combined with exploratory data analysis and dimensionality reduction.

---

## Solution

The project follows an end-to-end analytical workflow:

- Data cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Feature Engineering
- Feature Scaling
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- K-Means Clustering
- Cluster Interpretation
- Business Insights
